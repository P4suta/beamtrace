# Code of conduct

We want BeamTrace to be a precise, kind, and safe place to collaborate.

Be respectful, assume good intent while examining evidence, welcome different experience levels, and critique ideas rather than people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate privacy violations, and publishing another person's confidential data are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior threatens contributors, users, or the project. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with attention to the safety of the reporter.
